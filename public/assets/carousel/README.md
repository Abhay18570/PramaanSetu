The source renders were generated at 16:9 and converted to 1280 × 720 WebP files at quality 80. To replace an image, keep its filename and use a landscape image with a 16:9 crop at 1280 × 720 or larger. Keep important subjects away from the lower edge because the caption overlay occupies that area.
